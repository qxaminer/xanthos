What it does

LiDAR surface capture

Scans the geometry of any real skate spot — stairs, ledges, banks — and anchors AR content precisely to the surface.

Ghost skater overlay

A mocap-driven ghost figure appears on the actual surface in front of you. Watch the trick play out at the real location, at real scale.

Speed scrubber

Slow the trick from 2× down to 0.1× to study foot placement frame by frame. Keyframe markers highlight critical moments.

Spot discovery map

Community-tagged locations with attached trick libraries. Browse what's been landed at each spot before you arrive.

Injury risk display

Difficulty tier and injury risk shown before selecting a trick. Prerequisites surfaced up front — not after a fall.

Geo pointer

A live 3D arrow in AR points toward the nearest tagged skate spot, updating as you move.

Context

Final project for CRCP6380 Mobile Computing and AR, MS Creative Technology, SMU — Spring 2026. The v1 crawl demo uses primitive stand-ins for full assets. Ghost figures are RealityKit capsules; animations are procedural. Full mocap integration and community spot database are next-phase work.
