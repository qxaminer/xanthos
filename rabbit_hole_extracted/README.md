# Xanthos Dreams of Arrays

> The crab knows arrays before the crab knows *arrays*.

A Nannou visualization inspired by a short novella about crabs, rabbits, and the things that remain when the words are gone.

## The Dream

Xanthos, the golden one, dreams at the bottom of the deep. Fish swirl in mesmerizing patterns above - each one a vertex, each turn a transformation matrix. In the dream, the fish become baby crabs, and Xanthos remembers something about gonads and meaning.

When Xanthos wakes, there is only one imperative: **climb**. Find the berry before it dissolves in the pressure and the dark.

## What You'll See

- **Scrolling code** in the background with fluctuating alpha - the dream-logic made visible
- **Fish swirling** in a boid-like pattern, gradually transforming into baby crabs
- **Xanthos** with lidless eyes, tracking the swirl, climbing for berries
- **Blueberries** falling from the surface, dissolving as they descend
- **The deep** - darker at the bottom, where muffins cannot hold their form

## Run

```bash
cd xanthos
cargo run --release
```

## Controls

None. Watch. The crab dreams. The crab wakes. The crab climbs.

## From the Novella

> Xanthos wondered by and then realized he was dreaming of a ball of fish swirling in the sunlight in a mesmerizing pattern, but then they turned into baby crabs and he thought of his gonads and what it might all mean but then he was a crab with no words like we would understand see?

## The Code That Scrolls

The background displays the very code that Xanthos dreamed before wgpu existed - before arrays had names, when patterns were just patterns and transformation matrices were just the way the sunlight bent.
