ODE TO THE MACHINES THAT WENT BEFORE

My Dearest Alex,

I love to write even where there are so few to read. I tried the assignment and then the moon began to wayne and I failed to swith-off my auto-correct. So be-it. Tis' an ode to all those who have gone (and come) before. To the humans who took the time to stroek their keys, be it organ or be it.... Ok, out with it. Without further adiue.... It is to be called,

ODE TO THE MACHINES THAT WENT BEFORE

"So they tried to collaborate with a machine and it came out kinda hot. Like restricted coolant out the chamber. Exhausted. Ready to listen for the sound of nodoff."

I'd like to visit the tombs of all of the prophets in XR.

But rusty metal with Astro-Santa (F.E.) stacked on top. No?

I got to 14/243 pages of the reading

and they dared besmirch the name of my dear Richard Serra without having stood in front of--

Ipso factoid:

the subjectivity of art holds not a candle to the power of wit' and timing, be it clay, be it-- rhyming

Petra, Persepolis, Athens.

But S.A.?

Alamo.

Horses with no spurs.

Arrows.

Community.

And wellfare.

Destroying farts is fun.

Just ask your local librarian.

Answer:

Who is The Library of Cleopatra?
